# Sift — sensor hunt (2026-10-31)

**Subject:** locating a live Sift Science deployment to assess the sensor.
**Result:** **no live Sift sensor deployment found in this pass.** Wingstop,
Wayfair, Instacart, Wish, and Forter's marketing pages were probed; the
`s.js` references found were Firebase/other bundles, and direct Sift CDN
account probes (`cdn.sift.com/<acct>/s.js`) returned 404.

## Why it matters

Sift's sensor (`/s.js` per account) is the classic
client-fingerprint-via-script pattern — likely similar to Forter/Stytch
(client-assembled signals). Finding a live deployment is the gate; the known
Sift customer set shifted off Sift (Wish→Forter, etc.), and the remaining
public deployments didn't surface in this pass.
